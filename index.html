Research

My work examines the relationship between predictive performance and the interpretation of learned financial risk states.

Auditing Weakly Supervised Cryptocurrency Risk-State Learning: When Label Fit, Stability, and Semantics Disagree

Lou, J., Zhang, F., & Tian, S.

Manuscript

A three-axis audit of proxy-label agreement, temporal stability, and ex-post risk semantics using hourly data for five cryptocurrencies. Stronger label fit need not imply better risk stratification; the findings depend on the drawdown information encoded in the labels.

Research details
  • Temporal-model benchmarks on Bitcoin and Ethereum, with Transformer- and PatchTST-style stress tests on Bitcoin.
  • Baseline comparisons, ablations, label-design controls, and block-bootstrap analysis.
  • A Semantic Orientation Ratio to diagnose proxy-label misalignment, including XRP as a boundary case and explicit limits on general model ranking.

First author: study design, implementation, experiments, and manuscript preparation.
