About me

My research interests lie in weak supervision, financial time-series analysis, and model evaluation.

My recent work studies how to evaluate learned cryptocurrency risk states when proxy-label agreement, temporal stability, and economic meaning do not align. I am interested in understanding what model scores tell us—and what they leave unresolved.

I also build financial data applications and have experience in natural language processing, backend development, and data infrastructure.
